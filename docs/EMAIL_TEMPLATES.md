# Replying to Join Requests & Questions — Coach's Playbook

How to reply: in **Admin → Join Requests**, click the person's **email address**
— your mail app opens with the address pre-filled. Paste the closest template
below, adjust the `{...}` parts, send, then click the ✓ in the inbox to mark
the request as **read**.

Golden rules:
1. **Use their name and their level** in the first line — personal beats formal.
2. **One clear next step** per email (come to a trial session / fill this form / reply with X).
3. **Reply within 24–48h** — speed is the professionalism they remember.
4. Keep it short: 5–8 sentences maximum.
